I had a car accident on the first day of Golden Week. Once I fall asleep, I lose all memories of that day. I keep a diary to record, as much as possible, the memories that disappear every time I wake up.

This records everything that happened from May 5, 2019 up until yesterday. After waking up, make sure to read it through and remember it all.
